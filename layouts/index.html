The Problem

Documentation is stealing your clinical life.

⏱

Hours of after-hours charting

A 50-minute therapy session can demand 30–45 minutes of documentation. Across a full caseload, that's hours every evening — unpaid, exhausting, and the leading driver of clinician burnout.

👁

Split attention breaks the alliance

Note-taking during a session forces you to divide attention between the client in front of you and the chart you're building. Your clients feel it. Your therapeutic outcomes reflect it.

☁

Unsecure cloud scribes create data leaks

Legacy AI scribes store audio on generic third-party servers with unclear data retention policies. In behavioral health, unmanaged cloud processing risks exposing sensitive client records.

⚖ Substance abuse records fall under 42 CFR Part 2 — stricter than HIPAA. Kerrigan's zero-retention, end-to-end encrypted cloud pipeline guarantees full data sovereignty without hardware burden.

How It Works

One session. No charting. Clinical notes ready.

From audio capture to EHR-ready notes — powered by Kerrigan's secure, zero-retention cloud AI pipeline.

1

Record or connect

Connect to Kerrigan's secure cloud API from any browser or desktop app — no hardware setup or local servers required. Record in-person or connect via Zoom. No push-to-talk, no configuration. Just have a natural conversation.

2

High-accuracy cloud transcription

Cloud Whisper models process encrypted audio streams in real time with automatic speaker diarization separating clinician and client voices.

3

AI generates the note

Fine-tuned clinical LLMs in our secure cloud pipeline translate the transcript into a structured clinical note — SOAP, DAP, GIRP, or progress note format. Review, edit, and push to your EHR with one click.

4

Chat over the transcript

Ask clinically-grounded questions about the session. Surface key themes, risk indicators, or treatment plan updates. Context-aware AI that knows the full transcript — not just a snippet.
