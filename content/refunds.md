**Last Updated:** September 29, 2026

{{< legalcard title="1. Where this stands today" >}}
Self-serve signup is not open yet, and accounts are set up by hand. Until that changes, everything on this page is handled by a person through our [contact form](/#access).
{{< /legalcard >}}

{{< legalcard title="2. Trial" >}}
Every plan starts with a 14-day trial. We do not ask for a card to start it. When the trial ends, you are not charged unless you choose a plan.
{{< /legalcard >}}

{{< legalcard title="3. Plans" >}}
Billing is monthly only. Founder is $69.99 a month for the first ten clinicians, and that rate is held. Solo is $99.99 a month. Neither plan caps the number of notes you write.
{{< /legalcard >}}

{{< legalcard title="4. Renewals" >}}
We never charge you without your consent. Automatic renewal is never on by default; it happens only if you opt in. Before every renewal charge, we tell you in advance.
{{< /legalcard >}}

{{< legalcard title="5. Cancellation" >}}
Cancelling is no harder than signing up. Today you cancel the same way you sign up: send us a note through the [contact form](/#access). Billing, including cancellation, will move to your profile page in the app.
{{< /legalcard >}}

{{< legalcard title="6. Refunds" >}}
If you ask for a refund, you get one. Ask through the [contact form](/#access).
{{< /legalcard >}}
