# Business Associate Agreement

This Business Associate Agreement ("**BAA**") is entered into on the date Customer accepts it electronically through the Services, as LatentWorx's records show (the "**Effective Date**"), between:

- **LatentWorx, Ltd.**, a Delaware company ("**LatentWorx**"); and
- **[[CUSTOMER_LEGAL_NAME]]**, a [[CUSTOMER_ENTITY_TYPE_AND_STATE]] with its principal address at [[CUSTOMER_ADDRESS]] ("**Customer**").

LatentWorx and Customer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## Recitals

A. Customer is a covered entity under HIPAA or, where Customer is a part 2 program that is not a covered entity, the Parties apply this BAA to Customer by contract, as the QSOA provides. LatentWorx provides Kerrigan, an AI clinical documentation service, to Customer under the Underlying Agreement.

B. LatentWorx will create, receive, maintain or transmit Protected Health Information on Customer's behalf and is therefore Customer's business associate.

C. HIPAA permits Customer to share PHI with LatentWorx only on written assurances that meet 45 C.F.R. §§ 164.502(e) and 164.504(e). This BAA gives those assurances.

The Parties therefore agree as follows.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 **Incorporated terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this BAA, such as Business Associate, Covered Entity, Data Aggregation, Designated Record Set, Disclosure, Health Care Operations, Individual, Secretary and Use, have the meanings given in the HIPAA Rules. "**HIPAA**" means the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996, the HITECH Act, and their implementing regulations at 45 C.F.R. Parts 160 and 164 (the "**HIPAA Rules**").

1.2 **Defined terms.** In this BAA:

(a) "**Actual Knowledge Date**" means the first day on which LatentWorx's privacy and security contact named in Exhibit D, or the person then acting in that role, has actual knowledge of the matter to be reported.

(b) "**Breach**" has the meaning given in 45 C.F.R. § 164.402.

(c) "**business day**" means any day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a United States federal public holiday.

(d) "**Discovery**" means the first day on which a Breach is known to LatentWorx or, by exercising reasonable diligence, would have been known to LatentWorx, as determined under 45 C.F.R. § 164.410(a)(2).

(e) "**Electronic PHI**" means PHI that is "electronic protected health information" as defined in 45 C.F.R. § 160.103, that is, PHI transmitted by electronic media or maintained in electronic media.

(f) "**Part 2 Record**" means a record, as defined in 42 C.F.R. § 2.11, that Customer, as a part 2 program, discloses to LatentWorx, whether or not a QSOA is in force.

(g) "**PHI**" or "**Protected Health Information**" has the meaning given in 45 C.F.R. § 160.103, limited to the information LatentWorx creates, receives, maintains or transmits for or on behalf of Customer. PHI includes Electronic PHI.

(h) "**QSOA**" means any Qualified Service Organization Agreement between the Parties under 42 C.F.R. Part 2.

(i) "**Required by Law**" has the meaning given in 45 C.F.R. § 164.103.

(j) "**Security Incident**" has the meaning given in 45 C.F.R. § 164.304: the attempted or successful unauthorized access, use, disclosure, modification or destruction of information, or interference with system operations in an information system.

(k) "**Services**" means the services LatentWorx provides to Customer under the Underlying Agreement, as described in Exhibit A.

(l) "**Subcontractor**" means a person or entity to whom LatentWorx delegates a function, activity or service involving the creation, receipt, maintenance or transmission of PHI, other than a member of LatentWorx's workforce.

(m) "**Underlying Agreement**" means the LatentWorx Terms of Service at https://latentworx.com/terms, and any order forms or renewals under it.

(n) "**Unsecured PHI**" has the meaning given to "unsecured protected health information" in 45 C.F.R. § 164.402.

1.3 **Exhibits.** Exhibit A (Services and Data), Exhibit B (Safeguards), Exhibit C (Subcontractors) and Exhibit D (Incident and Breach Procedure) are set out after the Acceptance section below and form part of this BAA. Where the Parties have also signed the QSOA, the QSOA incorporates them by reference.

## 2. Permitted and Required Uses and Disclosures

2.1 **General rule.** LatentWorx will use and disclose PHI only as this BAA permits or requires, or as Required by Law (45 C.F.R. §§ 164.502(a)(3), 164.504(e)(2)(i)). LatentWorx will not use or disclose PHI in a way that would violate Subpart E of 45 C.F.R. Part 164 if Customer did it, except for the uses in Sections 2.3 and 2.4, which that Subpart permits a business associate contract to authorize.

2.2 **To perform the Services.** LatentWorx may use and disclose PHI as necessary to perform the Services described in Exhibit A for Customer.

2.3 **Management and administration.** LatentWorx may use PHI, if necessary, for its proper management and administration or to carry out its legal responsibilities (45 C.F.R. § 164.504(e)(4)(i)). LatentWorx may disclose PHI for those purposes only if (45 C.F.R. § 164.504(e)(4)(ii)):

(a) the disclosure is Required by Law; or

(b) LatentWorx first obtains reasonable assurances from the recipient that the PHI will be held confidentially and used or further disclosed only as Required by Law or for the purpose for which it was disclosed, and that the recipient will tell LatentWorx of any instance it learns of in which the confidentiality of the PHI has been breached.

2.4 **Data Aggregation (off unless requested).** LatentWorx will provide Data Aggregation services relating to Customer's Health Care Operations only if Customer asks for them in writing, and only within the scope of that request (45 C.F.R. § 164.504(e)(2)(i)).

2.5 **Minimum necessary.** LatentWorx will limit its uses, disclosures and requests of PHI to the minimum necessary to accomplish the intended purpose, consistent with 45 C.F.R. § 164.502(b) and Customer's minimum necessary policies where Customer has shared them with LatentWorx.

2.6 **Prohibited uses.** LatentWorx will not:

(a) sell PHI or receive any direct or indirect payment in exchange for PHI, except as 45 C.F.R. § 164.502(a)(5)(ii) permits (including payment for performing the Services);

(b) use or disclose PHI for marketing or fundraising;

(c) store or process PHI outside the United States, except as Section 5.4 provides; or

(d) use or disclose PHI for any purpose that this BAA does not permit or require.

2.7 **No model training.** LatentWorx will not use PHI to train, fine-tune or improve any model, whether its own or a third party's. Using PHI to operate, support, debug or secure the Services for Customer is not training, fine-tuning or improving a model. LatentWorx will engage a Subcontractor that processes PHI only under written terms that prohibit the same, as Exhibit C records for each Subcontractor.

2.8 **No de-identification.** LatentWorx will not de-identify PHI, or create any de-identified data set from PHI, unless the Parties first agree in writing.

2.9 **Legal process.** If LatentWorx receives a subpoena, court order or other legal demand for PHI, it will notify Customer within two (2) business days after receipt and, where legally permitted, before responding, so that Customer may seek a protective order or other remedy. LatentWorx will disclose only the PHI that the demand legally requires and only to the extent the disclosure is Required by Law.

2.10 **Substance use disorder records.** If Customer is a part 2 program, PHI that is also a Part 2 Record is also governed by the QSOA. Among other things, LatentWorx will not use or disclose those records to initiate or substantiate criminal charges against, or conduct a criminal investigation of, the patient, or in any civil, criminal, administrative or legislative proceeding against the patient (42 C.F.R. § 2.12(d)(1)), and will disclose them in response to legal process only under a court order that meets 42 C.F.R. § 2.64 or § 2.65, as the QSOA sets out.

## 3. Obligations of Business Associate

LatentWorx will:

3.1 **Use only as permitted.** Not use or further disclose PHI other than as permitted or required by this BAA or as Required by Law (45 C.F.R. § 164.504(e)(2)(ii)(A)).

3.2 **Safeguards.** Use appropriate administrative, physical and technical safeguards, and comply with the applicable requirements of Subpart C of 45 C.F.R. Part 164 (the Security Rule) with respect to Electronic PHI, to prevent use or disclosure of PHI other than as this BAA provides (45 C.F.R. §§ 164.504(e)(2)(ii)(B), 164.314(a)(2)(i)(A)). Exhibit B describes the safeguards LatentWorx operates as of the Effective Date; it is a description, and does not limit LatentWorx's obligations under this Section. LatentWorx may change its safeguards over time but will not materially reduce the overall protection of PHI described in Exhibit B.

3.3 **Report improper uses and disclosures.** Report to Customer any use or disclosure of PHI not provided for by this BAA of which it becomes aware, including Breaches of Unsecured PHI as required by 45 C.F.R. § 164.410, and any Security Incident of which it becomes aware, as set out in Section 4 (45 C.F.R. §§ 164.504(e)(2)(ii)(C), 164.314(a)(2)(i)(C)).

3.4 **Subcontractors.** Not permit a Subcontractor to create, receive, maintain or transmit PHI unless the Subcontractor has first agreed in writing to the same restrictions and conditions that apply to LatentWorx with respect to that PHI, and to comply with the applicable requirements of Subpart C of 45 C.F.R. Part 164, as set out in Section 5 (45 C.F.R. §§ 164.504(e)(2)(ii)(D), 164.502(e)(1)(ii), 164.308(b)(2), 164.314(a)(2)(i)(B), 164.504(e)(5)).

3.5 **Access.** Make PHI in a Designated Record Set available to Customer as necessary for Customer to meet its obligations under 45 C.F.R. § 164.524, as set out in Section 6 (45 C.F.R. § 164.504(e)(2)(ii)(E)).

3.6 **Amendment.** Make PHI in a Designated Record Set available for amendment and incorporate any amendments as Customer directs under 45 C.F.R. § 164.526, as set out in Section 6 (45 C.F.R. § 164.504(e)(2)(ii)(F)).

3.7 **Accounting.** Maintain and make available the information required for Customer to provide an accounting of disclosures under 45 C.F.R. § 164.528, as set out in Section 6 (45 C.F.R. § 164.504(e)(2)(ii)(G)).

3.8 **Customer's Privacy Rule obligations.** To the extent LatentWorx carries out any of Customer's obligations under Subpart E of 45 C.F.R. Part 164, comply with the requirements of that Subpart that apply to Customer in performing that obligation (45 C.F.R. § 164.504(e)(2)(ii)(H)). Unless Exhibit A says otherwise, LatentWorx carries out no such obligation.

3.9 **Books and records.** Make its internal practices, books and records relating to the use and disclosure of PHI available to the Secretary for purposes of determining Customer's compliance with the HIPAA Rules (45 C.F.R. § 164.504(e)(2)(ii)(I)). Where legally permitted, LatentWorx will notify Customer of any such request and give Customer a copy of what it provides. Nothing in this Section waives any privilege.

3.10 **Return or destruction.** At termination, return or destroy PHI, or extend this BAA's protections to PHI that cannot feasibly be returned or destroyed, as set out in Section 9 (45 C.F.R. § 164.504(e)(2)(ii)(J)).

3.11 **Workforce.** Train the members of its workforce who have access to PHI on the handling of PHI under this BAA within thirty (30) days after the Effective Date and on hire, and bind each of them to confidentiality obligations consistent with this BAA.

3.12 **Mitigation.** Take commercially reasonable steps, within LatentWorx's systems and control, to contain and mitigate, to the extent practicable, any harmful effect known to LatentWorx of a use or disclosure of PHI by LatentWorx or its Subcontractors in violation of this BAA. The costs of notifying individuals, or of offering services to them, are recoverable only under Section 10.1(c) and are subject to Section 10.2.

3.13 **Internal escalation.** Require its workforce members and Subcontractors to report any suspected Security Incident, Breach or improper use or disclosure of PHI to its privacy and security contact named in Exhibit D within one (1) business day of becoming aware of it.

## 4. Security Incident and Breach Notification

4.1 **Notice of Security Incidents.** LatentWorx will notify Customer of any Security Incident, other than an unsuccessful Security Incident described in Section 4.2, without unreasonable delay and no later than five (5) business days after the Actual Knowledge Date, or sooner where applicable law requires LatentWorx itself to give notice sooner, and in all cases no later than thirty (30) days after the date on which the Security Incident would be treated as discovered if the standard in 45 C.F.R. § 164.410(a)(2) applied to it. If the Security Incident is also a Breach of Unsecured PHI, Section 4.3 also applies.

4.2 **Unsuccessful Security Incidents.** Unsuccessful attempts occur routinely, such as pings, port scans, failed log-in attempts, denial-of-service attempts and blocked malware, that do not result in unauthorized access, use, disclosure, modification or destruction of PHI or interference with the information systems that hold it. This Section 4.2 is notice of them, and no further notice is required. On Customer's written request, made no more than once per calendar quarter, LatentWorx will provide a summary of unsuccessful Security Incidents for the period requested.

4.3 **Notice of Breaches of Unsecured PHI.** LatentWorx will notify Customer of any Breach of Unsecured PHI without unreasonable delay and no later than five (5) business days after the Actual Knowledge Date, or sooner where applicable law requires LatentWorx itself to give notice sooner, and in every case within the period 45 C.F.R. § 164.410 requires, measured from Discovery. The time limit in 45 C.F.R. § 164.410(b) is an outer limit; whichever period ends first governs.

4.3A **Other improper uses and disclosures.** LatentWorx will report to Customer any other use or disclosure of PHI not provided for by this BAA, whether or not it is a Breach, without unreasonable delay and no later than five (5) business days after the Actual Knowledge Date, and in all cases no later than thirty (30) days after the date on which the use or disclosure would be treated as discovered if the standard in 45 C.F.R. § 164.410(a)(2) applied to it.

4.4 **Content of notice.** LatentWorx's notice of a Breach will include, to the extent possible, the information required by 45 C.F.R. § 164.410(c): the identification of each individual whose Unsecured PHI has been, or is reasonably believed by LatentWorx to have been, accessed, acquired, used or disclosed during the Breach, and any other available information Customer is required to include in its notice to individuals under 45 C.F.R. § 164.404(c), such as what happened, the dates of the Breach and of Discovery, the types of PHI involved, and the steps LatentWorx is taking. LatentWorx will not delay notice to complete its investigation and will supplement the notice promptly as information becomes available. Exhibit D sets out the contacts, channels and notice template.

4.5 **Notifications to individuals, regulators and the media.** Customer will make any notifications to individuals, to the Secretary, to state regulators and to the media that the HIPAA Rules or state law require, unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing for a specific incident. LatentWorx will cooperate reasonably and promptly with Customer's investigation and notifications, including by providing information Customer reasonably requests. LatentWorx will not notify affected individuals or regulators about a Breach of Customer's PHI without Customer's prior written consent, unless it is Required by Law to do so.

4.6 **Law enforcement delay.** If a law enforcement official tells LatentWorx that a notice would impede a criminal investigation or damage national security, LatentWorx may delay notice to the extent the HIPAA Rules permit such a delay, and will tell Customer as soon as it lawfully can.

8.1 **Term.** This BAA starts on the Effective Date and ends when the Underlying Agreement ends, unless terminated earlier under this Section 8. LatentWorx's obligations under this BAA continue for as long as LatentWorx or any Subcontractor retains any PHI.

8.2 **Termination by Customer for cause.** If Customer determines that LatentWorx has violated a material term of this BAA, Customer may give LatentWorx written notice describing the violation (45 C.F.R. §§ 164.504(e)(2)(iii), 164.504(e)(1)(ii)). If LatentWorx does not cure the violation or end it within thirty (30) days after the notice, Customer may terminate this BAA and the Underlying Agreement by written notice. If cure is not possible, Customer may terminate immediately by written notice. If Customer terminates under this Section, LatentWorx will refund prepaid fees for the unused term.

8.3 **Termination by LatentWorx for cause.** If Customer violates a material term of this BAA, including by requiring a use or disclosure that Section 7.1 prohibits, LatentWorx may terminate this BAA and the Underlying Agreement on the same notice and cure terms as Section 8.2.

8.4 **Effect on the Underlying Agreement.** Because the Services cannot run without a BAA, termination of this BAA permits either Party to terminate the Underlying Agreement, and LatentWorx will stop receiving new PHI on that date.

## 9. Return and Destruction of PHI

9.1 **Return.** For thirty (30) days after this BAA terminates (the "**Return Period**"), LatentWorx will keep Customer's PHI available for Customer to access and copy through the Services. On Customer's written request made within the Return Period, LatentWorx will return to Customer, at no charge, all PHI that LatentWorx or its Subcontractors still maintain, in a format reasonably usable by Customer: an export of clinical notes and transcripts, and audio files if Customer asks for them.

9.2 **Destruction.** Customer has thirty (30) days after termination to export its PHI (the Return Period under Section 9.1). LatentWorx will complete destruction within thirty (30) days after that period ends, or sooner once Customer confirms in writing that export is complete, and in any case within thirty (30) days after that confirmation. Destruction covers all PHI held in LatentWorx's and its Subcontractors' active systems, is carried out by deleting Customer's records and stored objects in accordance with NIST SP 800-88 guidelines, and leaves no copies except as Section 9.3 permits. Copies in backups and snapshots are deleted as those backups expire, within thirty (30) days after active-system destruction, and remain protected under Section 9.3 until then. LatentWorx will give Customer a written certificate of destruction, signed by an authorized representative of LatentWorx, stating the date active-system copies were destroyed and the date by which backup copies expire.

9.3 **When return or destruction is infeasible.** If returning or destroying any PHI is infeasible, LatentWorx will tell Customer in writing what PHI is affected and why. LatentWorx will then extend the protections of this BAA to that PHI and limit further uses and disclosures to the purposes that make return or destruction infeasible, for as long as LatentWorx retains it (45 C.F.R. § 164.504(e)(2)(ii)(J)). The same applies to PHI that LatentWorx must keep to meet a legal obligation under Section 2.3, which LatentWorx will keep only as long as that obligation lasts and will then return or destroy.

9.4 **Deletion during the term.** Customer may ask LatentWorx in writing, or use any deletion function the Services make available to Customer, to delete specified audio recordings, transcripts or notes during the term. LatentWorx will complete a requested deletion from its active systems within thirty (30) days after the request and confirm it in writing. Backup copies are deleted as those backups expire, within the period stated in Section 9.2, and remain protected under Section 9.3 until then.

## 10. Liability, Indemnification and Insurance

(a) claims brought by third parties;

(b) civil monetary penalties imposed by the Secretary or a state attorney general, and other amounts paid to regulators; and

(c) the reasonable costs of forensic investigation, legal counsel for regulatory response, notifying individuals, regulators and the media of a Breach as required by law, call-centre support, and credit or identity monitoring offered to affected individuals,

in each case to the extent arising from the Indemnifying Party's breach of this BAA or violation of HIPAA or 42 C.F.R. Part 2, and excluding any portion caused by the indemnified Party's own breach, negligence, misconduct or instructions. For a third-party claim, the indemnified Party will give prompt written notice of the claim, let the Indemnifying Party control the defense and settlement (but no settlement may admit fault for, or bind, the indemnified Party without its consent), and cooperate at the Indemnifying Party's expense. Any investigation, audit or proceeding by the Secretary, a state attorney general or another regulator against the indemnified Party is controlled by the indemnified Party, at the Indemnifying Party's expense to the extent this Section 10.1 provides, subject to Section 10.2.

10.2 **Shared, mutual cap.** Each Party's total aggregate liability arising out of or relating to PHI or Part 2 Records under this BAA, the QSOA and the Underlying Agreement combined, including under Section 10.1, will not exceed the fees Customer paid to LatentWorx under the Underlying Agreement in the twelve (12) months before the first event giving rise to any liability. This cap is not additional to any cap in the Underlying Agreement: liability subject to it does not also count against, and is not also recoverable under, any cap in the Underlying Agreement.

10.3 **Exclusion of consequential damages.** Neither Party is liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or lost profits or revenue, arising out of or relating to this BAA, even if advised of their possibility. This Section 10.3 does not limit amounts payable under Section 10.1.

10.4 **Exceptions.** Nothing in this BAA limits either Party's liability for its fraud or willful misconduct. In this BAA, "**willful misconduct**" means intentional wrongdoing undertaken with knowledge that it is wrongful.

10.5 **Insurance.** Intentionally omitted.

## 11. Audit and Compliance

11.1 **Security questionnaires.** Once per contract year, on Customer's written request, LatentWorx will give written responses to a reasonable security and privacy questionnaire and copies of its current HIPAA privacy and security policies. Customer will treat them as LatentWorx's confidential information, except that Customer may share them with its legal and insurance advisers and with regulators.

11.2 **Regulator-required inspection.** LatentWorx will permit an on-site inspection of its relevant practices only if the Secretary or a state regulator requires Customer to conduct one. Customer will give at least thirty (30) days' written notice, bear its own costs and LatentWorx's reasonable costs, conduct the inspection during LatentWorx's normal business hours without unreasonably disrupting operations, and keep everything it learns confidential. ## Exhibit A — Services and Data

### 1. Services Description

1.1 LatentWorx provides Customer with Kerrigan, an AI clinical scribe for behavioral-health and substance use disorder ("**SUD**") clinicians. The Services consist of:

(a) live in-session audio transcription, performed on graphics processing unit ("**GPU**") infrastructure that LatentWorx operates inside its own AWS account using a self-hosted transcription model; audio is not sent to any third-party transcription API;

(b) clinical note drafting, in ASAM six-dimension, DAP, narrative and custom template formats, produced by large language model inference on AWS Bedrock, called from LatentWorx's AWS account;

(c) transcript-grounded clinical chat, allowing the clinician to ask questions about a session transcript;

(d) clinician-approved note revision with diffs, note versioning, and rollback to a prior note version; and

(e) clipboard-based export of a finished note for the clinician to paste into Customer's own electronic health record ("**EHR**") or other systems of record.

1.2 The Services can capture a Zoom session live and can import a Zoom session transcript. The Services do not include a native in-meeting Zoom integration.

1.3 An account is created when Customer completes sign-up through the Services, confirmed by LatentWorx's payment processor. The account holder signs in by a single-use link sent to the account email address. The Services do not today support a multi-provider organizational account model.

### 2. Categories of Protected Health Information and Part 2 Records Handled

2.1 In providing the Services, LatentWorx creates, receives, maintains or transmits the following categories of PHI and, where Customer is a part 2 program, Part 2 Records:

(a) **Audio**: recordings of clinical sessions captured live;

(b) **Transcripts**: text output of the transcription process, and session transcripts Customer imports;

(c) **Draft and final notes**: AI-drafted and clinician-approved clinical notes, including all saved versions;

(d) **Chat**: the clinician's questions and the Services' answers in transcript-grounded clinical chat;

(e) **Note-generation traces**: copies of the prompts sent for note generation and the model outputs returned, held in the trace store described in Section 3.1(i); and

(f) **Metadata**: session timestamps, note version history, user and account identifiers, and similar records describing the above.

2.2 **Other data (not PHI).** LatentWorx also holds clinician account information collected at onboarding, such as name and contact information. This is not PHI and is not governed by the BAA.

2.3 **Where records are kept relative to Customer's EHR.** Audio, transcripts, draft and final notes, every saved note version, chat and note-generation traces are stored in LatentWorx's systems, separately from Customer's EHR. A finished note reaches Customer's EHR only when a clinician copies it there. The copies in Kerrigan remain in LatentWorx's systems until they are deleted under BAA Section 9.4 or destroyed under BAA Section 9.2. Customer has thirty (30) days after termination to export its PHI. LatentWorx will complete destruction within thirty (30) days after that period ends, or sooner once Customer confirms in writing that export is complete, and in any case within thirty (30) days after that confirmation. The exception is note-generation traces, which expire as Section 3.1(i) describes.

2.4 Where Customer is a part 2 program, the categories in 2.1 that identify a patient as having received SUD diagnosis, treatment or referral for treatment are Part 2 Records and are handled under the QSOA as well as the BAA.

### 3. Data Flow

3.1 In plain language, data moves through the following systems:

(a) **Clinician's browser**: the clinician starts a live session or imports a session transcript.

(b) **AWS network**: the connection reaches LatentWorx's AWS account through AWS network services covered by LatentWorx's agreement with AWS (Exhibit C). Those services may end the connection's TLS encryption at the AWS location that receives it, and so handle the content of the request, including PHI, in unencrypted form there, and forward the request to the Services' ingress inside LatentWorx's cluster over a new encrypted connection.

(c) **LatentWorx AWS account**: the compute, storage and inference described in (d) to (i) run in a single-tenant AWS account controlled by LatentWorx.

(d) **Self-hosted transcription on GPU**: audio is transcribed by a self-hosted model running on GPU infrastructure in that AWS account.

(e) **AWS Bedrock inference**: the transcript is sent to AWS Bedrock, called from that AWS account, for note drafting.

(f) **Application database**: session, transcript, note, chat and metadata records are stored in a PostgreSQL database running inside LatentWorx's Kubernetes cluster, on Amazon EBS volumes encrypted with AWS Key Management Service ("**KMS**"). Access to each record through the application is mediated by a database row scoped to the customer.

(g) **Object storage**: audio files and related large objects are stored in an object store (MinIO) running inside LatentWorx's Kubernetes cluster, on Amazon EBS volumes encrypted with KMS.

(h) **Audit log**: administrative actions are written to an append-only audit log enforced at the database; access to notes is also logged.

(i) **LLM trace store**: copies of note-generation prompts and outputs are held in an in-cluster trace store (Langfuse), which keeps them in a ClickHouse database on Amazon EBS volumes and in a KMS-encrypted Amazon S3 bucket. A daily job deletes these copies once they are fourteen (14) days old.

3.2 **Data location.** LatentWorx stores and processes PHI and Part 2 Records only in AWS regions located in the United States (us-west-2 and, for AWS Bedrock inference, us-east-1), and requires each Subcontractor storing or processing such records to do the same. The AWS network services described in Section 3.1(b) may handle a connection, including in unencrypted form, at the AWS location that receives it, which is not limited to those regions and may be outside the United States. Transit of encrypted data, and connections a user initiates from outside the United States, are not storage or processing. LatentWorx will not move storage or processing outside the United States without Customer's prior written consent.

### 4. What LatentWorx Does Not Do

4.1 LatentWorx does not write data back into Customer's EHR. Note export is a one-way, clinician-initiated clipboard copy; LatentWorx has no integration that writes to Customer's EHR.

4.2 LatentWorx will not sell PHI or Part 2 Records, as "sale" is described at 45 C.F.R. § 164.502(a)(5)(ii).

4.3 LatentWorx will not use PHI or Part 2 Records for marketing.

4.4 LatentWorx will not use PHI to train, fine-tune or improve any model, whether its own or a third party's. Using PHI to operate, support, debug or secure the Services for Customer is not training, fine-tuning or improving a model. LatentWorx will engage a Subcontractor that processes PHI only under written terms that prohibit the same (BAA Section 2.7). LatentWorx will not use Part 2 Records to train, fine-tune or improve any model, whether its own or a third party's (QSOA Section 5.3). LatentWorx will not de-identify PHI, or create any de-identified data set from PHI, unless the Parties first agree in writing (BAA Section 2.8). LatentWorx will not de-identify Part 2 Records for any purpose of its own (QSOA Section 5.4).

#### 1.3 Technical Safeguards

(a) **Encryption at rest.** PHI at rest in the production systems listed in Exhibit A is encrypted with AWS KMS.

(b) **Encryption in transit.** Connections from users' browsers to the Services use TLS. AWS network services that carry the connection may end TLS at the AWS location that receives it and forward it to the Services' ingress inside LatentWorx's cluster over a new TLS connection (Exhibit A, Section 3.1(b); Exhibit C). (d) **Admin audit log.** Administrative actions are written to an append-only audit log enforced at the database. The log cannot be altered or deleted through the application or by application administrators. This control is not represented as preventing changes by LatentWorx personnel with database-owner privileges.

(e) **Note access logging.** Access to clinical notes is logged.

(f) **Deletion controls.** Deletion of records is actor-attributed, reason-recorded and gated behind a break-glass procedure.

(g) **AI inference location.** Note-drafting inference runs on AWS Bedrock, called from LatentWorx's AWS account. Bedrock is an AWS-operated service governed by AWS's terms (Exhibit C).

(h) **Transcription location.** Transcription runs on GPU infrastructure that LatentWorx operates in its AWS account, not on a third-party transcription vendor's infrastructure.

(i) **Note versioning.** Notes are versioned, so prior states of a note are preserved and recoverable rather than overwritten.

(j) **LLM trace expiry.** Copies of note-generation prompts and outputs held in the in-cluster trace store are deleted by a daily job once they are fourteen (14) days old.

(k) **Backups.** Backups and snapshots that contain PHI are retained for no more than thirty (30) days and then expire and are deleted.
