# Qualified Service Organization Agreement (42 C.F.R. Part 2)

This Qualified Service Organization Agreement (this "**QSOA**") is entered into on the date the Program accepts it electronically through the Services, as LatentWorx's records show (the "**Effective Date**"), between:

- **LatentWorx, Ltd.**, a [[OWNER_LATENTWORX_STATE]] company, [[OWNER_LATENTWORX_ADDRESS]] ("**LatentWorx**"); and
- **[[CUSTOMER_LEGAL_NAME]]**, a [[CUSTOMER_ENTITY_TYPE_AND_STATE]], [[CUSTOMER_ADDRESS]] (the "**Program**").

LatentWorx and the Program are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## Background

A. The Program operates a federally assisted substance use disorder ("**SUD**") program and is a "part 2 program" under 42 C.F.R. Part 2.

B. LatentWorx provides the Program with Kerrigan, an AI clinical documentation service, under the LatentWorx Terms of Service at https://latentworx.com/terms (the "**Underlying Agreement**"). The service is data processing: it transcribes clinical sessions, drafts notes from those transcripts, stores the results and logs access to notes. Doing this requires LatentWorx to receive, store and process records the Program holds about its patients.

C. Under 42 C.F.R. § 2.11, a person that provides data processing or other services to a part 2 program, and that signs a written agreement making the acknowledgements in Section 3 of this QSOA, is a "qualified service organization." The Parties sign this QSOA so that LatentWorx is one.

D. Where the Program is also a HIPAA covered entity, LatentWorx is also its business associate under the Business Associate Agreement between the Parties, accepted electronically through the Services (the "**BAA**"). Paragraph (3) of the "qualified service organization" definition in 42 C.F.R. § 2.11 extends qualified service organization status to a business associate of a part 2 program that is also a covered entity, for protected health information that is also a Part 2 record. The Parties intend LatentWorx to be a qualified service organization on both grounds where both apply, and on the grounds in paragraphs (1) and (2) of that definition in every case.

E. The Parties have drafted this QSOA to the text of 42 C.F.R. Part 2 as amended by the final rule published February 16, 2024 (89 Fed. Reg. 12612), whose compliance date of 2026-02-16 has passed.

## 4. Services and Scope of Disclosures

4.1 **Services.** LatentWorx provides the Services described in Exhibit A: transcription of live sessions and import of existing session transcripts, AI drafting and clinician-approved revision of clinical notes, transcript-grounded clinical chat, storage, versioning and rollback of notes, clipboard export of notes by the clinician for entry into the Program's own record system, and logging of access to notes.

4.2 **Basis for disclosure.** The Program discloses Part 2 Records to LatentWorx under 42 C.F.R. § 2.12(c)(4), which removes Part 2's restrictions on use and disclosure from communications between a part 2 program and its qualified service organization of information the organization needs to provide services to or on behalf of the program. No separate patient consent is required for those communications. That exception applies only because LatentWorx has made the acknowledgements in Section 3; it covers only information LatentWorx needs to provide the Services; and it does not authorize any further disclosure by LatentWorx, which is limited to Section 5.1.

4.3 **Minimum needed.** The Program will send, and LatentWorx will request and use, only the Part 2 Records LatentWorx needs to provide the Services. LatentWorx will not request Part 2 Records beyond what a clinician captures or enters in the Services.

## 5. Use and Disclosure of Part 2 Records

5.1 **Permitted uses and disclosures.** LatentWorx will use and disclose Part 2 Records only:

(a) to provide the Services to the Program, including any disclosure the Program directs in writing under Section 7.1;

(b) to the Subcontractors listed in Exhibit C, each acting for LatentWorx in providing the Services to the Program and bound as Section 8 requires, and only to the extent they need the records to support the Services. The Program authorizes these disclosures;

(c) as Required by Law, and then only in compliance with Section 6; and

(d) to LatentWorx's legal counsel, forensic investigators and insurers, only as needed to investigate, respond to or defend a Security Incident, Breach or claim, and only after each has agreed in writing to be bound by Part 2 for those records.

5.2 **No other redisclosure.** LatentWorx will not redisclose any Part 2 Record, or any information identifying a person as a current or former patient of the Program, to any other person, including a patient's family, employer, insurer or a government authority, except as Section 5.1 allows.

5.3 **No model training.** LatentWorx will not use Part 2 Records to train, fine-tune or improve any model, whether its own or a third party's. Using Part 2 Records to operate, support, debug or secure the Services for the Program is not training, fine-tuning or improving a model. LatentWorx will engage a Subcontractor that processes Part 2 Records only under written terms that prohibit the same.

5.4 **No de-identification.** LatentWorx will not de-identify Part 2 Records for any purpose of its own.

5.5 **Data location.** LatentWorx stores and processes Part 2 Records only in AWS regions located in the United States, and requires each Subcontractor storing or processing such records to do the same. Transit of encrypted data, and connections a user initiates from outside the US, are not storage or processing. LatentWorx will not move storage or processing outside the US without the Program's prior written consent.

5.6 **No use against patients.** LatentWorx will not use or disclose any Part 2 Record, or testimony describing its contents, to initiate or substantiate criminal charges against a patient or to conduct any criminal investigation of a patient, except under a court order meeting 42 C.F.R. § 2.65, consistent with 42 C.F.R. § 2.12(d)(1), which attaches that restriction to the record in the hands of whoever obtains it. More broadly, LatentWorx will not use or disclose any Part 2 Record in any civil, criminal, administrative or legislative proceeding against the patient unless the patient has consented in writing or a court has authorized it by an order meeting 42 C.F.R. § 2.64 or § 2.65. This Section survives termination without limit of time, and LatentWorx remains responsible for it for Part 2 Records in a Subcontractor's possession.

## 6. Legal Demands and Judicial Proceedings

6.1 **No disclosure without a qualifying court order.** A subpoena, warrant or other Legal Demand is not by itself enough to compel LatentWorx to disclose a Part 2 Record. LatentWorx will not disclose any Part 2 Record in response to a Legal Demand unless (a) a court has issued an order meeting 42 C.F.R. § 2.64 (non-criminal purposes) or § 2.65 (criminal investigation or prosecution of a patient), or Part 2 otherwise expressly permits the disclosure, and (b) unless the law or the order forbids that notice, the Program has been notified under Section 6.2. A disclosure compelled from LatentWorx or a Subcontractor by process that cannot lawfully be refused, made after LatentWorx has asserted Part 2 under Section 6.3, is not a breach of this QSOA.

6.2 **Notice to the Program.** LatentWorx will notify the Program in writing of any Legal Demand within two (2) business days after receiving it, unless the law forbids that notice. The notice will include a copy of the Legal Demand and the response deadline, so that the Program can respond, seek a protective order or take part in any proceeding under 42 C.F.R. § 2.64 or § 2.65.

6.3 **Baseline steps at LatentWorx's cost.** In meeting Section 3.2, LatentWorx will, at its own cost:

(a) decline to produce any Part 2 Record unless Section 6.1 permits it;

(b) object in writing to the issuing party, citing Part 2;

(c) notify the Program under Section 6.2; and

(d) if a party applies to a court for an order under 42 C.F.R. § 2.64 or § 2.65, file a written objection asserting Part 2.

LatentWorx will cooperate reasonably with the Program's own response and will disclose only what any qualifying order requires.

6.4 **Steps beyond the baseline.** Where a step beyond Section 6.3 is necessary to meet Section 3.2, including a court appearance, motion or appeal, LatentWorx will take it, acting reasonably after consulting the Program. The Program reimburses LatentWorx's reasonable outside-counsel fees for any step beyond that baseline, except where the demand arises from LatentWorx's own breach. ## 10. Audits and Evaluations (42 C.F.R. § 2.53)

10.1 **Records available to the Program.** For a management audit, financial audit or program evaluation under 42 C.F.R. § 2.53, the Program will first use the Services' own export features. On the Program's written request, LatentWorx will provide any further Part 2 Records it holds for the Program, in a reasonable electronic form and within a reasonable time, at the Program's reasonable cost.

10.2 **Auditors reviewing Part 2 Records held by LatentWorx.** LatentWorx will allow an auditor or evaluator to review Part 2 Records while they are in LatentWorx's possession only if the Program has authorized the review in writing and has confirmed to LatentWorx in writing each of the following.

(a) **The track.** Whether the review is (i) a review without the records being downloaded, copied, removed or forwarded to another electronic system or device (42 C.F.R. § 2.53(a)); (ii) a review in which records are copied, removed, downloaded or forwarded (42 C.F.R. § 2.53(b)); or (iii) a Medicare, Medicaid or Children's Health Insurance Program audit or evaluation (42 C.F.R. § 2.53(e)).

(b) **Who the auditor acts for.** An "**Eligible Sponsor**" is: (i) a federal, state or local governmental agency that provides financial assistance to the Program or is authorized by law to regulate its activities; (ii) a person that provides financial assistance to the Program, that is a third-party payer or health plan covering patients in the Program, or that is a quality improvement organization performing a quality improvement organization review, or the contractors, subcontractors or legal representatives of such a person or organization; or (iii) an entity with direct administrative control over the Program. The Program must confirm:

  - for track (a)(i), that the auditor performs the audit or evaluation on behalf of an Eligible Sponsor, or that the Program has determined that the auditor is qualified to conduct an audit or evaluation of the Program;
  - for track (a)(ii), that the auditor performs the audit or evaluation on behalf of an Eligible Sponsor; a qualification determination by the Program is not enough on this track; and
  - for track (a)(iii), that the review is a Medicare, Medicaid or Children's Health Insurance Program audit or evaluation and, where it is conducted for a CMS-regulated accountable care organization or similar CMS-regulated organization, that the conditions of 42 C.F.R. § 2.53(e)(3) are met.

(c) **The auditor's written agreement.** That, before the review, the auditor agreed in writing: on track (a)(i), to comply with the limits in Section 10.2(d); on tracks (a)(ii) and (a)(iii), to maintain and destroy the Patient Identifying Information consistently with the policies and procedures established under 42 C.F.R. § 2.16, to retain records in compliance with applicable federal, state and local record retention laws, and to comply with the limits in Section 10.2(d). The Program, not LatentWorx, obtains and keeps that agreement on file, and gives LatentWorx a copy before the review.

(d) **The limits on the auditor (42 C.F.R. § 2.53(e) and (f)).** On tracks (a)(i) and (a)(ii), two separate limits bind the auditor, and both apply (42 C.F.R. § 2.53(f)):

  - **Disclosure limit:** the auditor may disclose the Patient Identifying Information only back to the Program; and
  - **Use limit:** the auditor may use it only to carry out the audit or evaluation, or to investigate or prosecute criminal or other activities as authorized by a court order entered under 42 C.F.R. § 2.66.

  On track (a)(iii), neither limit above applies; 42 C.F.R. § 2.53(e)(5) and (e)(6) govern instead. The auditor may not use or disclose the Patient Identifying Information for any purpose other than those necessary to complete the audit or evaluation, and may further use or disclose it to its contractors, subcontractors or legal representatives to carry out the audit or evaluation.

10.3 **Cost.** Review under Section 10.2 that LatentWorx must support beyond giving access through the Services is at the Program's reasonable cost.

10.4 **Audits of LatentWorx itself.** Audits of LatentWorx's own controls, rather than of the Program's records, are governed by BAA Section 11, including its frequency and cost limits.

## 11. Record Classification and SUD Counseling Notes

11.1 **The Program decides.** The Program, not LatentWorx, decides whether any record it creates in the Services is a Part 2 Record, whether it is an SUD Counseling Note, and which consent applies to it.

11.2 **The test.** Under 42 C.F.R. § 2.11, a record is an SUD Counseling Note only if all three of the following are true:

(a) it is recorded, in any medium, by a provider of the Program who is an SUD or mental health professional;

(b) it documents or analyzes the contents of conversation during a private SUD counseling session or a group, joint or family SUD counseling session; and

(c) it is kept separated from the rest of the patient's SUD and medical record.

Even then, the definition does not include medication prescription and monitoring, counseling session start and stop times, the modalities and frequencies of treatment furnished, results of clinical tests, or any summary of diagnosis, functional status, the treatment plan, symptoms, prognosis or progress to date.

11.3 **Consent and LatentWorx's handling.** LatentWorx does not designate any feature of the Services for SUD Counseling Notes, and treats records in the Services as part of the patient's ordinary clinical record unless the Program notifies LatentWorx in writing that it will use the Services for SUD Counseling Notes. If the Program does so, the Program is responsible for obtaining the separate patient consent 42 C.F.R. § 2.31(b) requires for any use or disclosure of SUD Counseling Notes that needs it. That consent cannot be combined with consent for any other records except another consent for SUD Counseling Notes, and a single consent for treatment, payment and health care operations does not cover them. LatentWorx will not use or disclose a record the Program has identified as an SUD Counseling Note except to provide the Services to the Program.

11.4 **Deletion after export.** On the Program's written instruction, LatentWorx will delete the session transcript and draft notes for a session once the clinician has exported the final note, within the period and subject to the backup terms BAA Section 9.4 sets for deletion requests during the term. Encrypted backups expire on their normal schedule, within thirty (30) days after active-system destruction.

## 13. Term, Termination, Return and Destruction

13.1 **Term.** This QSOA starts on the Effective Date and continues until the BAA terminates or this QSOA is terminated under Section 13.2. The Sections listed in Section 15.3 then survive as that Section provides.

13.2 **Termination.** Either Party may terminate this QSOA on the grounds, and by the process, BAA Section 8 sets for termination of the BAA, applied to breaches of this QSOA. Termination of the BAA terminates this QSOA, and termination of this QSOA for a material breach is grounds to terminate the BAA. On termination LatentWorx stops receiving new Part 2 Records.

13.3 **Return and destruction.** The Program has thirty (30) days after termination to export its Part 2 Records. LatentWorx will complete destruction within thirty (30) days after that period ends, or sooner once the Program confirms in writing that export is complete, and in any case within thirty (30) days after that confirmation. On termination, LatentWorx will return or destroy Part 2 Records as BAA Section 9 provides, so that, except as Section 13.4 permits, no Part 2 Record remains with LatentWorx or any Subcontractor. LatentWorx will give the Program the certificate described in BAA Section 9.2, identifying the Part 2 Records destroyed and any held under Section 13.4.

13.4 **Where return or destruction is infeasible.** If returning or destroying a Part 2 Record is infeasible, LatentWorx will tell the Program which records and why. It will keep protecting them under this QSOA and limit any further use or disclosure to the purpose that makes return or destruction infeasible, for as long as it holds them. Return or destruction is infeasible, and this Section applies, for:

(a) entries in append-only audit and access logs, and disclosure records LatentWorx keeps to meet BAA Section 6.3 or another legal obligation, for the period that obligation requires, after which LatentWorx will destroy them; and

(b) encrypted backups, until they expire on LatentWorx's normal backup cycle, within thirty (30) days after active-system destruction.
